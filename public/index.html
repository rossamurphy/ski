How to Play Stock Ski
  • Pick a stock — search any ticker or company up top, or pick a preset & timeframe.
  • ✏️ Draw on the chart — sketch bridges, ramps, and catch-curves right over the price line so your skier survives drops and clears climbs.
  • 📐 Move Ramp — drag the blue on-ramp anywhere on the chart to drop in wherever you want.
  • Smooth, Tilt & Zoom — use the top-right sliders to smooth or tilt the chart downhill, pinch/scroll or use + / − to zoom, Space to Play/Pause, R to Reset.
